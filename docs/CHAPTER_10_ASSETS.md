# Chapter X Asset List: "The Lobster Quadrille"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Draft for the owner. Plates checked against web-resolution images from Victorian Web on 2026-10-10. The prompts are in [ART_PROMPTS.md](ART_PROMPTS.md), "Chapter X asset list", and are added to [ASSETS_MASTER.md](ASSETS_MASTER.md). Same method as before: the Alice model sheet attached for every Alice drawing; Tenniel plates as references, and cut out directly for figures other than Alice (from high-resolution scans).

## The chapter in places
1. **The seashore** (Chapter IX's set, `src/sets/seashore.js`), all chapter long: the Gryphon and the Mock Turtle describe the Lobster Quadrille, capering wildly, then dance it solemnly round and round Alice while the Mock Turtle sings; the talk of whitings and porpoises; Alice tells her adventures and recites "'Tis the voice of the Lobster" (it comes out wrong); the Mock Turtle sings "Beautiful Soup"; "The trial's beginning!" and the Gryphon hurries Alice off by the hand.

## Colour
Only words in the poems and songs (COLOR.md rule 6): "turn not pale" (pale), "You have baked me too brown" (brown), "Beautiful Soup, so rich and green" (green). Each word is coloured in the text strip. Staged as well: the Lobster in his vignette is baked brown, and the soup in the tureen vignette is green. Everything else is ink.
