## Scene
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| The Lobster Quadrille (`quadrille`) | "The Mock Turtle sighed deeply" | The Gryphon thumps the sobbing Mock Turtle on the back. The Mock Turtle climbs down from his ledge; as they describe the dance the Gryphon bounds into the air and the Mock Turtle capers wildly, then both sit down sadly. Alice stands in the open sand and they dance solemnly round and round her while he sings (her toes trodden on). Then all three sit, one on each side of her, for the whitings, the boots and shoes, and her adventures. She stands to recite, sits with her face in her hands, recites again. The Mock Turtle sings "Beautiful Soup", sobbing; at "The trial's beginning!" the Gryphon hurries Alice off and the Mock Turtle is left singing alone. Parts: A Lobster Quadrille; Will you walk a little faster?; Whitings, boots and shoes; 'Tis the voice of the Lobster; Beautiful Soup; The trial's beginning! |

Thought vignettes: lobsters thrown out to sea; the whiting, the snail and the porpoise; whitings with their tails in their mouths; the Lobster at his glass (brown); the Owl and the Panther; Beautiful Soup in its tureen (green).
