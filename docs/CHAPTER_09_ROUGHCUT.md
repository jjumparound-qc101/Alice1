# Chapter IX Rough Cut: "The Mock Turtle's Story"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Plays start to finish (14 minutes) in three scenes (`src/scenes/mockTurtle.js`), with placeholders drawn in code (`src/props9.js`; the cards from `src/props8.js`).

## Scenes
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| The Duchess and her morals (`duchessWalk`) | "You can't think how glad I am to see you again" | On Chapter VIII's croquet-ground. Alice and the Duchess walk arm in arm; the Duchess squeezes closer and digs her chin into Alice's shoulder (three times). The Queen walks up and stands in front of them with her arms folded; the Duchess trembles; the Queen stamps; the Duchess runs. The players rest in the shade under the tree, hurry back, and are led off into custody one by one while the arches disappear. "You are all pardoned." Parts: 'Tis love that makes the world go round; Flamingoes and mustard; Frowning like a thunderstorm; All in custody |
| The Gryphon (`gryphon`) | "They very soon came upon a Gryphon" | A grassy bank in the sun. The Gryphon asleep; the Queen orders it up and walks off; it sits up, rubs its eyes, chuckles, and walks off with Alice behind |
| The Mock Turtle (`mockTurtle`) | "the Mock Turtle in the distance" | The seashore (`src/sets/seashore.js`, shared with Chapter X). The Mock Turtle on his ledge of rock; they sit down; his sobbing and the Gryphon's "Hjckrrh!"; Alice ready to sink into the earth; the Gryphon's paws up in surprise; counting subjects on his flappers; both hide their faces. Parts: Once I was a real Turtle; School in the sea; Reeling and Writhing; Lessons |

Thought vignettes: pepper, vinegar, camomile and barley-sugar; pigs flying; school in the sea; the conger-eel and the crab.

Colour: none (the chapter names no colours).
