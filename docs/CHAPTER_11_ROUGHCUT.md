# Chapter XI Rough Cut: "Who Stole the Tarts?"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Plays start to finish (10 minutes) in one scene (`src/scenes/court.js`) in the new court set (`src/sets/court.js`, shared with Chapter XII), with placeholders drawn in code (`src/props11.js`, plus earlier chapters' figures).

## Scene
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| The court of justice (`trial`) | "The King and Queen of Hearts were seated on their throne" | The back wall with its canopy of hearts; the King (as judge: crown over wig) and the Queen on the dais; the White Rabbit as herald beside the King; the Knave in chains between two soldiers; the dish of tarts in the middle; the jury-box with twelve creatures writing on slates (Bill the Lizard fourth); crowds of birds, beasts and cards behind rails; the door in the right-hand wall. Alice and the Gryphon at the front. The King's spectacles at "Silence in the court!"; Alice goes round behind the jury-box and takes Bill's squeaking pencil; the herald's trumpet and scroll. The Hatter comes in with his teacup and bread-and-butter (the March Hare behind him, the Dormouse beside Alice); turns pale under the Queen's spectacles; bites his teacup; Alice sits and begins to grow; the Dormouse crosses the court; the Hatter shakes his shoes off, goes down on one knee, and hurries out, leaving the shoes. Each guinea-pig cheers and is put into a canvas bag with an officer sitting on it. The cook at the witness stand; the King folds his arms; "Treacle!"; the Dormouse turned out; "Alice!" Parts: The jury; Herald, read the accusation!; The first witness; Alice grows; The guinea-pigs; The cook; "Alice!" |

Thought vignettes: tarts handed round as refreshments; "stupid things!" on the slates.

Colour (COLOR.md): the Hatter's face turns pale (rule 3); the Rabbit's pink eyes. The card people's suits are red and black, as on real cards (owner, 2026-10-11); the officers of the court are the Three and Four of Spades.

## Scale
Alice is a foot high at first. From "she was beginning to grow larger again" she grows to about twice that by the end of the chapter (Chapter XII continues: "how large she had grown").
