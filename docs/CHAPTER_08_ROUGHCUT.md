# Chapter VIII Rough Cut: "The Queen's Croquet-Ground"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Plays start to finish (13 minutes) in two scenes (`src/scenes/croquet.js`), with placeholders drawn in code (`src/props8.js`). Both scenes play in the Queen's garden, `src/sets/croquet.js`, which Chapter IX shares.

## Scenes
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| Painting the roses red (`roseTree`) | "A large rose-tree stood near the entrance of the garden" | Two, Five and Seven paint the white roses red one by one (each rose is its own card, so the red goes on as they work). They bow to Alice, then throw themselves flat; the procession passes two by two (ten soldiers, ten courtiers, ten children jumping hand in hand, the guests with the White Rabbit, the Knave with the crown on its crimson cushion, the King and Queen) and stops opposite Alice. The Queen's face flushes crimson at "Off with her head!"; "Nonsense!"; the Knave turns the gardeners over with one foot; they jump up and bow to everybody; Two on one knee. The procession moves on, three soldiers stay behind, and Alice hides the gardeners in the large flower-pot. Alice joins the procession and walks with the Rabbit, who whispers on tiptoe. Parts: The Queen! The Queen!; The procession; Off with her head!; The flower-pot; Walking with the Rabbit |
| The croquet-ground (`croquet`) | "Get to your places!" | The ridged and furrowed ground, soldiers doubled up as arches that wander off, players running about. Alice with her flamingo under her arm; it twists round to look up at her; the hedgehog unrolls and crawls away. The Queen stamps about. The Cheshire Cat's head appears in the air: the grin, then the eyes, then the whole head. The Queen listens behind Alice; the King comes up, gets behind her, and hurries off for the executioner. The flamingo flaps at a tree; two hedgehogs fight. A crowd round the Cat; the executioner goes off "like an arrow"; the Cat's head fades; the Duchess arrives; the King and the executioner run up and down. Parts: Managing her flamingo; The Cheshire Cat; A cat may look at a king; The flamingo in the tree; Off with its head? |

Thought vignette: tulip-roots instead of onions.

Colour (docs/COLOR.md): the roses (white, painted red), the crimson cushion, the Queen's crimson flush. The card people are coloured like real cards: red hearts and diamonds, black clubs and spades, period pip layouts with no numbers; each is a different card of one pack (owner, 2026-10-11).

## Scale
Alice stays "about a foot high" from the end of Chapter VII; the cards are about her height and the garden is built around them.

## Placeholders still in use
Every card: Two, Five and Seven (painting, bowing, kneeling, running, lying face down and face up), the Queen (standing, pointing, crimson, stamping), the King, the Knave with the cushion, the executioner, the soldiers, courtiers, children and guests; the soldier-arch; the rose-tree and roses, paint-pot, large flower-pot, garden wall and ridged ground; the flamingo (held, twisting, flying), the hedgehog (rolled, crawling); the Cat's head (cut from Chapter VI's placeholder Cat); the vignette. Scene-only figures (the procession and the players) are "extras" (`src/crowd.js`).

## Known gaps
- The flamingo is small and hard to read until it is drawn.
- The procession's figures slide with a hop; the drawn walk poses will make it read better.
- Players don't carry flamingoes or hit hedgehogs; the game is suggested by movement only.
