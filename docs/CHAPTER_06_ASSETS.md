# Chapter VI Asset List: "Pig and Pepper"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Draft for the owner. Plates checked against web-resolution images from Victorian Web on 2026-10-10. The prompts are in [ART_PROMPTS.md](ART_PROMPTS.md), "Chapter VI asset list". Same method as before: the Alice model sheet attached for every Alice drawing; Tenniel plates as references, and cut out directly for figures other than Alice (from high-resolution scans).

## The chapter in places
1. **Outside the little house** (Chapter V's clearing; Alice is nine inches high, the house four feet, so its people are her size): the two Footmen and the great letter; the plate that skims out at the Footman's head.
2. **The kitchen**, "full of smoke from one end to the other": the Duchess on a three-legged stool nursing the baby, the Cook at the fire with the cauldron, the Cheshire Cat on the hearth; the shower of fire-irons, saucepans, plates and dishes; the lullaby.
3. **The wood**: Alice carrying the baby, which turns into a pig and trots away; the Cheshire Cat on a bough, vanishing and reappearing, and at last fading tail-first until only the grin is left.
4. **In sight of the March Hare's house** (the last lines): chimneys shaped like ears, a roof thatched with fur. Chapter VII's set.

## Colour
None. Chapter VI names no colours; everything is ink (docs/COLOR.md).

## The Cheshire Cat's vanishing
Done in the game, not drawn: the Cat fades "beginning with the end of the tail, and ending with the grin, which remained some time after the rest of it had gone." For that the Cat needs a **separate grin** drawing (CC6-04), lined up with the face in CC6-02, so the body can fade tail-first while the grin stays.
