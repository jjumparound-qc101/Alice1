# Chapter XII Rough Cut: "Alice's Evidence"
Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Plays start to finish (12 minutes) in two scenes (`src/scenes/evidence.js`), with placeholders drawn in code (`src/props12.js`, plus earlier chapters'). With this chapter the whole book is staged.

## Scenes
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| Alice's evidence (`evidence`) | "Here!" cried Alice | In Chapter XI's court. Alice, grown large, jumps up and the jury-box tips over; the jurors fly out onto the crowd; she puts them back one by one, Bill head downwards with his tail waving until she sets him right. She walks out before the throne. "Rule Forty-two" from the King's note-book; she keeps growing; the King turns pale; the Rabbit jumps up with the paper and reads the verses; the Knave shakes his head; the King points to the tarts; the Queen throws the inkstand at Bill; "Sentence first"; the Queen turns purple; Alice at full size: "You're nothing but a pack of cards!" The whole pack (every card of it) rises into the air, swirls, and flies down upon her. Parts: Rule Forty-two; The verses; Sentence first; A pack of cards |
| The bank (`waking`) | "found herself lying on the bank" | Chapter I's riverbank. Alice leaning against her sister, dead leaves fluttering down; "Wake up, Alice dear!"; she runs off to tea. Her sister sits on and dreams: the White Rabbit hurries by, and faint figures appear around her as the text names them (the Mouse, the Hatter, the March Hare and the teapot, the Queen, the Duchess and the pig-baby, the Gryphon, the Lizard, the guinea-pigs, the Mock Turtle), then fade back into the countryside one by one (teacups into sheep-bells, the Queen into the shepherd boy, the baby and the Gryphon into the farm-yard, the Mock Turtle into the cattle). Last, Alice grown up, telling the dream to other children. Part: The sister's dream |

Thought vignettes: the globe of goldfish; the countryside sounds; Alice grown up.

Colour (COLOR.md): the King's face pale and the Queen's purple (rule 3); the card people red and black (owner, 2026-10-11); the Rabbit's pink eyes.
