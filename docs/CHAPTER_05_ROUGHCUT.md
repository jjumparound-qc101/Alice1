# Chapter V Rough Cut: "Advice from a Caterpillar"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Plays start to finish (12 minutes) in three scenes (`src/scenes/caterpillar.js`). The Father William poem is shown in Tenniel's own four plates (owner's choice); everything else new is a placeholder drawn in code (`src/props5.js`).

## Scenes
| Scene | From | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| Who are you? (`mushroom`) | "The Caterpillar and Alice looked at each other…" | Three-inch Alice below the mushroom, the blue Caterpillar on top. She turns away and comes back. Parts: Keep your temper; You are old, Father William; One side and the other |
| Father William | "Repeat, 'You are old, Father William'" | Tenniel's four plates, on paper mounts, turn in one per question and answer |
| One side and the other | "it got down off the mushroom, and crawled away" | The Caterpillar rears up, yawns, crawls off; she breaks a bit off each side, nibbles, and shrinks till her chin strikes her foot |
| A sea of green leaves (`treetops`) | "Come, my head's free at last!" | Her head on an immense neck rising out of green treetops (green per COLOR.md); the neck zigzags down "like a serpent"; the Pigeon flies into her face, then settles on her nest. Part: Serpent! |
| A little house (`clearing`) | "her right size again" | Her right size among trees; the little house four feet high; down to nine inches |
