## What happens in the house
- Inside: the table in the window, the bottle by the looking-glass; she drinks and grows, stoops, kneels and lies down until she fills the room. Thought vignettes: Dinah as a nurse; a book about Alice.
- Outside: her arm hangs out of the window and snatches twice; the Rabbit, then Pat, fall into the cucumber-frames (they break). The cart crowd arrives, the ladders go up, Bill climbs to the chimney; a kick, and Bill flies up and comes down by the hedge, to be held up by two guinea-pigs.
- The house shakes when she trembles; the door rattles when the Rabbit tries it.
- The pebbles land on the floor and turn into cakes; she eats one, shrinks, and runs out past the crowd.
