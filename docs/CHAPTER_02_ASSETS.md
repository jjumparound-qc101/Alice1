# Chapter II Asset List: "The Pool of Tears"
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Draft for the owner. Plates checked against web-resolution images from Victorian Web on 2026-10-10. The prompts are in [ART_PROMPTS.md](ART_PROMPTS.md), "Chapter II asset list".

## How Chapter II art is made (owner, 2026-10-10)
The same way as Chapter I: Tenniel plates where they exist, ChatGPT for the rest, **in the same conversation** as the Chapter I art so Alice and the Rabbit stay the same characters.

**One problem, and a recommendation.** Our Alice is the AI turnaround, and she doesn't look quite like Tenniel's Alice (face, hair, proportions). If a Tenniel Alice is cut out of a plate and placed next to ours, she will read as a different girl. The Victorian Web images are also small (432 px tall), too small for cutouts at game size.
- **Recommended:** use the Tenniel plates as **attached references** for ChatGPT redraws of *Alice's* poses, so she stays one character. Cut **other figures** straight from the plates (the Rabbit, as in Chapter I; the Mouse) once a high-resolution scan is found.
- This keeps "plates where they exist" for every pose a plate shows, and the source and disclosure are logged in PROVENANCE.md either way.
